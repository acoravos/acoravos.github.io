New York City is home base, though you'll often find me in Dublin, Ireland.

I'm a Venture Partner at [Pillar VC](https://www.pillar.vc/), backing and building companies in brain health and neurotech, frontier hardware, women's health, and longevity. Pillar partnered with the UK's Advanced Research + Invention Agency (ARIA) to launch [Encode: AI for Science](https://encode.pillar.vc/), a £10.9 million programme backing AI/ML engineers as they advance frontier science and build new ventures. I'm hiring a venture associate in the UK to work with me.

I'm an Expert-in-Residence (XIR) at [ARPA-H](https://arpa-h.gov/), the Advanced Research Projects Agency for Health, through Digitalis Commons. I focus on [DIGIHEALS](https://arpa-h.gov/research-and-funding/programs/digiheals), the Digital Health Security initiative, designed to protect the U.S. health care system's electronic infrastructure from cyberattacks.

I also run Grey Heron Global, a strategic advisory working with founders and execs, VC funds, and research orgs.

Before that, I co-founded HumanFirst and served as CEO for seven years, building the infrastructure for measuring health with digital tools in clinical research. In 2024 we sold the company to [ICON](https://www.statnews.com/2024/02/29/icon-acquires-humanfirst-digital-tools-clinical-trials/) (NASDAQ: ICLR).

I served on the Board of Directors of [VisionSpring](http://www.visionspring.org) for 10 years, helping hundreds of thousands of people get access to quality eye care.

Before HumanFirst, I served as an [EIR at the FDA](https://rockhealth.com/create-meaningful-change-in-healthcare-serve-a-tour-of-duty-in-government/) in the Digital Health Unit, and before that, a software engineer at [Instacart](https://tech.instacart.com/our-first-engineering-intern/) and [Akili Interactive](http://www.akiliinteractive.com/), the developer of the [first FDA-cleared video game](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-permits-marketing-first-game-based-digital-therapeutic-improve-attention-function-children-adhd).

Before transitioning into engineering, I worked at KKR Capstone and McKinsey, where I focused on the healthcare, energy, and financial industries. Duke Grad. [Birder](https://bsky.app/profile/jay.bsky.team/post/3kpzw7iisxs24). Co-founded [CODE@HBS](https://codehbs.github.io/), the computer science and engineering organization at Harvard Business School.

My all-time favorite podcast is John O'Donohue and Krista Tippett's conversation on [The Inner Landscape of Beauty](https://onbeing.org/programs/john-odonohue-the-inner-landscape-of-beauty/).

Past orgs and projects are over in [projects](/projects/).
