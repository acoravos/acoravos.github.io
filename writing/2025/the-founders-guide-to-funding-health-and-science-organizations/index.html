New collaboration with Astera Institute on funding your idea

A few months ago, Cate Hall asked me: “where’s the fundraising handbook that guides scientists through how the game actually works? And not the sanitized version VCs publish on Twitter.” The information asymmetry between founders and investors is real. VCs negotiate term sheets daily. You might do it a few times in your career. We’re flipping that script. Our 50+ page handbook was born from Cate’s question.

My co-author Rachel Katz and I have had time to reflect since we individually raised venture funding and sold our respective companies to public companies. We interviewed top entrepreneurs and nonprofit leaders to bring these whisper network lessons directly to you.

The things that somehow never make it into polite fundraising guides:
- A 101 guide to entity types and sources of capital for today’s environment
- Tools for building momentum, even if you’re running out of runway
- The hierarchy of investor introductions
- Whisper network tactics on negotiating term sheets
- Working back from your exit: how investors will get paid (and how employees can too)

This work stands on the shoulders of giants. For invaluable fundraising conversations and perspectives that shaped this handbook, big thank you to Elliot Cohen, Michael Esquivel, Lisa Marrone, Sumner L Norman, Wes Panek, Brian C. Patterson, Will Patrick, Conor Russomanno, Zach Weinberg, Carolyn Witte (and many more founders who shared their insights on background) for your thoughtful engagement with these ideas.

The editorial refinement of this work would not have been possible without Christina Agapakis and Ramya Mahalingam, whose careful attention to detail and clarity significantly enhanced the manuscript, and Michela Caffrey for skillful layout design. Any shortcomings remain entirely our own.

I’m grateful to Astera Institute for supporting open-access work that challenges the status quo — and for giving me a chance to reflect on the past few years and pay it forward.

We wanted to give you tools, not rules. Our goal: help you get funding efficiently so you can focus on getting world-shifting health and science innovations to market faster. Keep going. We’re rooting for you.
