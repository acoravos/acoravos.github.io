New collaboration with Astera Institute on selling software to pharma and biotech companies

In the early days of HumanFirst, structuring commercial contracts was one of our biggest challenges — how should we set pricing, protect IP, and package our software and services products. I was fortunate to have founder friends and other execs share their MSAs, order forms, and customer agreement templates, which proved invaluable.

During my sabbatical, I’ve had the opportunity to reflect on our early commercial decisions. Together with Abby DeVito (our Head of GTM at HumanFirst), Shu Hu (our counsel at Gunderson who advises the fastest growing life sciences startups), and Astera Institute, we’ve created the comprehensive guide we wished we’d had from day one.

This handbook would have saved me months (if not years) of iteration to establish solid agreements — the true foundation of strong customer relationships. We’re now sharing these early-stage learnings as an open-access resource for founding teams navigating pharma and biotech SaaS partnerships for the first time: https://astera.org/pharma-sales-handbook/.

If you’re an early-stage founding team selling SaaS into pharma or biotech, check out this open-access resource. Our goal: help you avoid months of painful iteration so you can focus on getting life-saving innovations to market faster.
